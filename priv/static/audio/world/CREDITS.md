# Bacon World sounds

All effects are CC0 (public domain) recordings. Most cuts come from the GodotFall project's
assets/audio (https://github.com/bonesvt/GodotFall), which trims and levels the original packs;
the female voice is taken straight from its pack, and the grass steps are mixed here from the
original takes (Fantozzi's grass/sand steps over a soft heel thump from Kenney's Impact Sounds).
build_audio.py converts them to MP3 with small gain changes. CC0 needs no credit, but here are
the people who recorded them.

## 2026-10-25: the second sound pass (build_audio_v2.py)

All of it is CC0 (public domain): recordings and music from OpenGameArt.org (each page's
licence checked as CC0 when it was fetched, 2026-10-25) and kenney.nl, cut, layered, filtered,
levelled and looped by `library/pipeline/scripts/build_audio_v2.py`. Lasers, the photon torpedo,
spell charges and shimmers, bells, wing beats, drones and the beds' tones are synthesised there
from scratch. CC0 needs no credit; these are the people who made the sources.

## 2026-11-02: no more nature noises; much more music

The owner: "remove all ambient sounds ... they ALL are terrible. Just give me a more diverse set of ambient
background music, no more nature noises." Deleted: the whole `ambient/` folder (the procedural rain, wind,
water, fire and wildlife set of 2026-10-31), the nature beds `amb_day`, `amb_birds`, `amb_wind`,
`amb_campfire`, `amb_lair`, `amb_high` and the details `sw_bird_*`, `sw_leaves_*`, `sw_gust_*`, `sw_drip_*`
(the rows above for them are history). The music now plays nearly back to back.

Added below: 55 more tracks (day 8-27, night 4-23, lair 4-13, boss 2-6), all from OpenGameArt.org, each page's licences checked on 2026-11-02 to include
CC0 (some pages offer CC0 alongside CC-BY/OGA-BY; we take them as CC0). Levelled to -21 LUFS, 80 kbit/s
stereo, at most 5 minutes; short loops repeated to 2.5-4 minutes (`tmp/b8work/music/`: plan.json,
convert.sh, loop.sh, shrink.sh).
